# Surface: portfolio home (/tr, /en)

Scope: the bilingual landing page and its project case-study sections. Visitor mode: Experience (the work leads from the first viewport) with a Persuade close (contact / CV).

Audience & job: consulting clients first, recruiters second (see PRODUCT.md). Action: "Let's talk" (contact) and "CV". Proof: real projects, labelled Personal / Client work / Corporate-internal; corporate screens are rebuilt mocks with anonymised data.

Approved comp: .impeccable/mocks/comp-2.png ("Mürekkepten İsim": the name combed out of the ink, the O of OĞULCAN is the ring lens onto Coach Panel, the red vein curls to Let's talk). Decision comp: .impeccable/mocks/decision/ebru/mono.png.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The site is a live ink-marbling (ebru) bath and the work surfaces through it; it refuses the dark bento developer portfolio with a gradient hero.

OWN-WORLD: Ink ebru only: black, white and graphite marbling with a single vermilion vein (#e5322b) reserved for the action and the active state. A heavy geometric grotesk name sits knocked out of a white band so the marble shows through the letters. White and graphite pills form the chrome, with no other hue.

STORY: Within one viewport the visitor learns who he is (Full-Stack & AI Engineer) and sees real work rise out of the bath. Scrolling surfaces each project (Coach Panel, Framely, Falaxy, Gemini Live, Black Books & Coffee, AI Influencer…) as a ring opening onto its screen. The visitor understands he ships interface, backend and AI end to end, then contacts him or takes the CV.

FIRST VIEWPORT: As in the decision comp. A nav pill top-centre with TR/EN. A white band across the upper third carries the full-width knockout name, with the "Full-Stack & AI Engineer" line beneath. A graphite vortex is centred, and its ring lens shows the live Coach Panel screen captioned "01 — Coach Panel". The red vein runs from the vortex toward the "Let's talk" red pill at bottom-right. A bottom pill bar lists the projects. Signature interaction: the cursor combs the live WebGL marbling, and scroll drops ink that blooms into the next project's ring. Motion grammar: fluid drag, ring bloom, pinned scenes, and no scattered hover effects.

FORM: Ebru (grounded candidate 3 of 7, the assigned direction), palette variant "Mürekkep" chosen by the user. seed:24a93321a191d238
